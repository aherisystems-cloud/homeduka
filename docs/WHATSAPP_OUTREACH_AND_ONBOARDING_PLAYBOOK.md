# WHATSAPP OUTREACH & SUPER-FAST ONBOARDING PLAYBOOK (ENGLISH SPECIFICATION)

> **Mission:** Convert active Kenyan advertisers (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp business groups) into live marketplace vendors with **zero form-filling**.  
> **Core Principle:** "Tell us what you want to achieve, share whatever links or catalog you already have, and our automated engine does all the heavy lifting."

---

## 1. The Strategy: Outcome-Driven English Outreach

Kenyan business owners are pragmatic and busy. If you send them a link to "Register an account" or "Fill out this form," drop-off is over 95%.

Instead, we speak directly to their bottom line:
1. **Acknowledge their work:** Prove you've seen their actual products.
2. **Present the value proposition:** Direct customer visibility from serious buyers in Nairobi and Kiambu at **zero cost**.
3. **Eliminate effort:** Tell them they don't need to fill forms; just share an existing link or catalog.
4. **Low-friction call to action:** A simple "Yes" to get their storefront built.
